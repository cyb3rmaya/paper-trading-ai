# Paper Trading AI — Project Summary

## 1. Service

An AI-powered financial education and paper trading platform for young learners.

- Learn economics and financial concepts through AI-generated short-form video content
- Progress through quests and missions
- Apply learned concepts through paper trading simulations
- Do not reward simulated investment returns directly, to discourage short-term speculation
- Use AI to analyze decision reasoning, misconceptions, and weak areas
- Recommend personalized next lessons and quests
- Provide parents with financial literacy and progress reports
- Freemium model with premium subscription
- Designed for self-directed learning

## 2. Core Value

This is not simply a paper trading game or an AI video service.

> Learners make financial decisions and explain why they made them. AI analyzes their reasoning, identifies misconceptions, and helps them build better financial decision-making skills.

Paper trading is the practice environment. The actual goal is to improve financial judgment and literacy.

## 3. Key Problems to Solve

- Measure real learning while preventing skip-farming
- Convert free users into paid subscribers
- Determine whether a single trial-end performance card is enough to drive subscription conversion
- Verify whether physical or offline rewards can scale economically
- Address the gap between simulated learning and real-world asset building from a parent's perspective
- Prevent learners from dropping out because financial education feels boring
- Avoid designs that encourage speculative trading behavior among minors

Learning should be measured through a combination of:

- Concept understanding
- Application
- Decision reasoning
- Review and retention
- Risk-management behavior

Video watch time should be treated as a supporting signal rather than the main learning metric.

## 4. Core Learning Loop

Short concept lesson → Quest → Financial decision → Explain reasoning → AI analysis and feedback → Result → Next personalized lesson

The service should focus on this loop before expanding into a full-scale platform.

## 5. MVP

The first MVP should remain intentionally small.

### Essential features

- Student account
- A small set of financial education content
- Quests and missions
- Simple paper trading or portfolio simulation
- Decision-reason input
- AI feedback
- Parent progress report

### Lower-priority features for later stages

- Real-time stock prices
- Large-scale rankings
- Physical rewards
- Complex multi-agent AI architecture
- Fully automated mass AI video generation

## 7. Current Goal

Government support programs and fundraising are intentionally excluded from the current stage.

The immediate objective is:

> Validate whether parents and young learners actually need this product, then build the smallest MVP that solves the validated problem.

3. Research the market and competing services

5. Validate the core hypotheses
